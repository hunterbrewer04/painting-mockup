The process

Slow is a schedule, not an accident.

Five stages, in order, each allowed the time it asks for. Nothing on a Vermeil project is hurried into place.

01

Consultation

We walk the rooms with you, in the light they actually live in. Scope and honesty first, before a single price is spoken.

02

Specification

A written finish schedule — colours, sheens, systems, cure times. Nothing on site is improvised, and nothing is left to memory.

03

Preparation

Protection, repair, fill, sand, prime. Nine-tenths of the hours no one ever sees, spent making the wall quieter than the paint to come.

04

Application

Small master crews, thin coats, long cures. Brushwork where brushwork belongs, and a straight roller where it does not.

05

The Reveal

Furniture returned to the millimetre, a final walkthrough together, and an aftercare dossier you keep for the life of the finish.
